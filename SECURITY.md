# Reporting a security problem

Every Harmony host publishes its security contact at `/.well-known/security.txt` (RFC 9116), together with an
expiry date and the languages we read (English, Arabic). Please write to that address rather than opening an
issue: issues are visible to everyone who can see the repository.

What helps us act quickly:

- the host and page where you saw the problem, and the time (UTC);
- what you did and what happened, step by step;
- the response headers or screenshots you have, with any session cookie values removed.

We acknowledge reports within five working days and keep you informed until the problem is closed. Please give
us a reasonable time to fix it before you describe it publicly, and never test against hotel data that is not
yours.
